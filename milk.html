Our milk

We collect milk twice a day, in the morning and again in the evening, on our three routes out of Ngong. Every delivery is tested at the collection centre and cooled straight away, so the milk stays fresh on its way to the buyer.

Farmers are paid once a month for every accepted litre they deliver. The sacco pays per accepted litre, so the more good milk you deliver, the more you earn.

What makes good milk

  • Use clean churns, washed after every use.
  • Deliver on time, morning and evening, so the milk does not stand.
  • Keep the milk cool and covered on the way to the centre.
  • Do not add water or anything else to the milk.

Good milk keeps our buyers happy, and happy buyers mean a steady price for every member.
